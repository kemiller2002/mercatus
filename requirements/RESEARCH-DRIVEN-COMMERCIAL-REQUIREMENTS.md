# Research-Driven Commercial Requirement Addendum

## BuyingGroup and internal alignment

ID: `RDC-01`
Add BuyingGroup with roles including Champion, EconomicBuyer, TechnicalEvaluator, Practitioner, RiskReviewer, LegalReviewer, Procurement, ExecutiveApprover, Blocker, Referrer, and UnknownInfluencer. One person may hold multiple roles. Roles may be unknown. Champion approval must not imply organizational approval.

InternalAlignment states: Unknown, Fragmented, Emerging, Aligned, Blocked, NotRequired. Alignment must be evidence-backed. Missing material stakeholders or disagreements create obligations.

## Forwardable proof

ID: `RDC-02`
Sales/proof assets should survive internal forwarding without presenter context and identify problem, evidence, reasoning, limitations, implication, and next action.

## Candidate AI Value Diagnostic

ID: `RDC-03`
Model a candidate productized commercial motion that captures strategic objective, use-case inventory, owner, direct/indirect cost, baseline, expected/observed outcome, measurement quality, workflow effect, revenue/client/quality/risk effects where relevant, counterfactual, reversibility, dependencies, evidence quality, and a Clarity scale/stop/change decision.

## Candidate Before You Rewrite Diagnostic

ID: `RDC-04`
It must be capable of concluding replace, incrementally modernize, isolate/contain, remediate process/architecture instead, gather more evidence, or do nothing yet. A diagnostic that always recommends modernization is invalid.

## Candidate AI System Diagnostic

ID: `RDC-05`
Evaluate business objective, system boundary, model/provider, context/data, prompts/instructions, state management, tool/effect boundaries, evaluation, failure modes, human verification, latency, token/model cost, retries, observability, privacy/security constraints, generated-code/maintenance effects, dependency/lock-in, and outcome measurement.

## TriggerFamily

ID: `RDC-06`
Support reusable trigger families including GrowthStress, ComplianceChange, CostReductionMandate, NewBusinessRequirement, PeerReferral, CapitalDecision, LeadershipChange, AcquisitionIntegration, AIInvestmentReview, RecurringFailure, FailedImplementation, ModernizationDecision, AccessibilityFailure, Incident, Audit, LitigationOrInvestigation, and Other. Measure effectiveness by population and offering.

## Problem-led thought leadership

ID: `RDC-07`
Each strategic asset should identify target ProblemPattern, BuyingGroup roles, challenged assumption, evidence, useful conclusion, proof level, and next commercial experiment. Generic topical content without a research/commercial hypothesis should be lower priority.

## Internal-forwarding journey

ID: `RDC-08`
Track evidence that a champion distributes material internally. Generate candidate obligations such as CreateFinanceProof, CreateTechnicalProof, AddressRiskConcern, ClarifyImplementationPath, and ProvideOutcomeEvidence.

## Competitive substitution tests

ID: `RDC-09`
Record what the buyer would actually use if Echelon did not exist: do nothing, internal team, generic AI/chat, manual documents/spreadsheets, existing platform, incumbent/specialist consultant, traditional RCA/ADR/decision matrix, or custom software. Willingness to switch is distinct from problem recognition.

## Wedge versus platform

ID: `RDC-10`
Distinguish the narrow wedge problem capable of acquiring a customer from the broader portfolio vision. Track which wedges create expansion.

## Time-to-value

ID: `RDC-11`
CommercialMotion must estimate and later measure time to first useful output, time to decision, time to measurable outcome, and founder/delivery effort.

## Deliverable reuse

ID: `RDC-12`
Decompose reusable governed method/template components from confidential client evidence.

## Evidence-of-value follow-up

ID: `RDC-13`
Every paid diagnostic should define expected outcome change, intervention owner, observation window, evidence to collect, and relevant confounders before completion.

## Disqualifying conditions

ID: `RDC-14`
Every commercial motion must define poor-fit conditions such as predetermined conclusion, unavailable evidence, no intention/authority to act, consequence too small for diagnostic cost, compromised independence, expertise outside scope, or timeline incompatible with responsible work. The system should make saying no efficient.
